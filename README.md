Blood-dependent organism stats use continuous formulas rather than lookup tables. Circulating volume continuously limits pressure, oxygen capacity, perfusion, pulse, and cardiac output, reaching zero only with zero blood. Sustained severe hypovolemia also develops a smooth probabilistic cardiac-collapse hazard instead of using a lethal cutoff: uncommon complications begin through the upper danger band, risk rises noticeably between 3000 and 2750 mL, and then accelerates sharply toward very common collapse at deep blood loss. Hypotension lowers consciousness to a severe but non-OTRUB floor, while the resulting cerebral oxygen loss owns unconsciousness and anoxic brain injury. Tissue O2 is coupled to perfusion and causes stamina, movement, disorientation, and fake-up impairment without directly changing consciousness or requesting OTRUB.

External wounds report only the blood loss they actually apply. Ordinary wound loss is mildly increased, while arterial wounds are substantially more hemorrhagic and their separate O2 penalties remain secondary to their rapid blood loss. Blood volume never starts the fixed incapacitation timer directly; cerebral oxygen, critical brain, airway, spinal, and cardiac failures—including probabilistic hypovolemic cardiac collapse—own that state.

Dying modes 6 (`fuck.mp3`) and 10 (`itshopeless.mp3`) share persistent playback channels with OTRUB modes 6 and 7, respectively, so losing consciousness changes volume without restarting the track. The Homigrad weapon base keeps a strong per-shot muzzle displacement and a shared, damped recoil tail: the gun rises and wanders during recovery, the live muzzle ray follows that displaced transform, and automatic fire accumulates physical climb instead of relying on screen shake. Weapon weight, cartridge impulse, stance, support hands, and arm effectiveness control the response; fractures and dislocations sharply increase recoil motion and time-to-aim, with a destroyed normal firing arm also paying a left-hand transition penalty. Respiration and arrest are reserve-based: loss of intake or circulation drains existing tissue, body, perfusion, and brain-oxygen states over time instead of zeroing or sharply capping them, while back armor with `underwaterOxygen = true` (currently the aqualung) continues feeding the current O2 model.

Hypothermia uses a central-survival response from 35 C toward full effect near 30 C: shivering initially raises oxygen demand, deeper cooling reduces it, and blood flow is diverted away from limbs and whole-body perfusion to preserve cerebral and myocardial oxygen. This response never bypasses failed breathing, absent circulation, traumatic penalties, or genuinely depleted volume; deep-cold arrhythmia and arrest remain lethal risks.

Hemothorax progression is owned by `sv_lungs.lua`: thoracic organ damage guarantees a slow pleural complication, while unrelated internal bleeding gets one severity-scaled risk roll per bleeding episode. Concussion, its post-concussion tail, and the related tinnitus, headache, fatigue, and nausea now use the accelerated recovery curve in `sv_trauma.lua`. The nausea target and pending pool must decay after active concussion ends so the symptom loop cannot persist indefinitely. All immediate head-trauma sources must use its shared capped addition helper so persistent brain or skull damage cannot strand concussion above the recovery model's maximum. Severe pain also has a continuous, resistance-aware syncope risk in addition to its slower shock-driven consciousness loss.

## Antique pistol source asset

The reference-matched exterior-only antique bolt-action pistol asset is under `antique_pistol/`. Its 14,712-triangle animated GLB, static OBJ, improved 2K wood/steel texture sources, Source SMD/QC/VMT handoff, collision mesh, reproducible Python builder, validation report, and directly mountable `gmod_addon/` test package are kept together there. The actual runtime files are spread into this add-on's standard `models/`, `materials/`, and `lua/` folders, including the spawnable `weapon_antique_pistol` SWEP and clientside preview commands. The model contains six sequences: the five source clips plus the combined runtime `cycle`. Its normalized `AnimsEvents` timing calls `RejectShell` about 0.42 seconds into that cycle using the existing `.357 Magnum` ammo definition and its current `10mm` physical-casing alias.

Startup sound validation is handled by `lua/autorun/sh_zcity_asset_scanner.lua`. Three seconds after each server and client startup it scans literal `.wav`, `.mp3`, and `.ogg` references in mounted Lua files, prints missing sounds to the console, and writes the full realm-specific report to `garrysmod/data/zcity_asset_scan/latest_server.txt` or `latest_client.txt`. Run `zcity_asset_scan` to rescan the current realm and `zcity_asset_scan_report` to print the latest report path.
